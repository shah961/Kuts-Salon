Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Introduction

Kuts & More Unisex Salon ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is handled when you visit our website or interact with our appointment inquiry forms.

2. Information We May Collect

When you fill out an appointment request or contact form on our website, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, requested salon service, and preferred appointment dates. Our website does not currently process online financial payments directly.

3. How Information Is Used

Information submitted through our website is strictly used to process appointment scheduling, respond to customer inquiries, and provide confirmation calls regarding your visit at our Mumbai salon.

4. Cookies & Web Analytics

We may utilize standard, non-intrusive browser cookies or performance analytics tools strictly to evaluate site loading speeds, page visits, and general navigation performance. No sensitive personal data is collected via analytical cookies.

5. Third-Party Links & Services

Our website contains links to external navigation services (such as Google Maps). We do not exercise control over third-party practices and recommend reviewing their respective privacy guidelines when leaving our site.

6. Data Protection & Retention

We maintain appropriate basic administrative safeguards to protect any submitted contact details from unauthorized access. Contact information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill salon communications and operational booking records.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You may request correction or deletion of any contact information you submitted by reaching us directly via telephone at 022 2307 0281 or emailing kutsandmore@hotmail.com.

8. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
