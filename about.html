About Kuts & More

A salon space dedicated to individualized hair, skin, and grooming care in Central Mumbai.

SALON PHILOSOPHY

Refined Personal Grooming

At Kuts & More Unisex Salon, we view hair care and skincare as an extension of personal confidence.

Located conveniently at Navjeevan Society on Lamington Road, our salon is structured to offer a welcoming, modern aesthetic space where clients can relax while receiving tailored beauty services.

Whether you visit us for routine maintenance, modern hair coloring, restorative scalp treatments, or skin glow facials, our goal remains consistent: delivering quality results using professional styling techniques.
